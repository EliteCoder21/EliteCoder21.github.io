## Overview

Alitheon builds visual-fingerprint authentication, which recognises an individual physical object from the fine detail on its surface. I worked there as a machine learning research intern in summer 2024 and summer 2026.

The specifics of the research are proprietary, so this page stays high level. You can read my reflections in [My summer at Alitheon](/posts/summer-at-alitheon/) and [Notes to a future intern](/posts/notes-to-a-future-intern/).

## What I worked on

- **Camera capture and calibration**, so that images from different stations are consistent and comparable
- **Recognition and data quality**, including learning how far a labelled dataset can be trusted
- **Object identity across cameras**, with an emphasis on careful, honest evaluation
- **Getting research into production**, including porting components so they run inside the team's own systems

## Skills

- Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, C++
- Computer vision, representation learning, OCR, zero-shot classification
- Evaluation design, benchmarking, technical writing, cross-team communication
